## Why a garden

The vacant lot on Maple Street has sat unused for six years. A community
garden would turn it into shared green space, and the soil survey from last
spring found no contamination.

## Plan

1. Confirm the lease terms with the city
2. Recruit ten founding members
3. Build the first eight raised beds

Materials we already have:

- Lumber for **four** beds
- A donated water tank
- Hand tools from the *street festival* fund

## Budget

The startup cost is `$2,400`, covered by:

```text
beds      1200
soil       600
fencing    400
signage    200
```

Questions about insurance are still open, and the neighbours on the north
side have not been consulted yet.
